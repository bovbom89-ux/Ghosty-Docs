GETTING STARTED

Configuration

Keep your security settings clear, deliberate and easy for staff to manage.

Recommended approach

  • Start with conservative protection settings.
  • Make sure trusted staff know how to trigger and reverse lockdowns.
  • Configure logging if the feature is available.
  • Review account-age and spam thresholds before enabling automatic actions.
  • Check your settings after updating the bot.
PROTECTION

Anti-Raid

Learn how raid protection can help your team respond to waves of joins or disruptive activity.

What to configure

  • Decide which activity should trigger an alert.
  • Choose a response, such as staff notification or lockdown, if supported.
  • Ensure staff know how to review events and restore normal access.
  • Check that the bot has the necessary Discord permissions.
Test automatic actions before relying on them in a live community.

Anti-Spam

Guidance for reducing repetitive messages and disruptive chat activity.

Good practices

  • Set limits that fit your server's normal pace.
  • Exempt trusted bots and channels only where appropriate.
  • Review logs before changing settings after a false positive.
  • Make sure staff understand what happens after repeated violations.
PROTECTION

Verification

Use a clear verification process to help new members access your community.

Plan your flow

  • Explain what members need to do and why.
  • Keep instructions short and accessible.
  • Provide a staff contact for members who get stuck.
  • Test the complete process with a regular member account.
PROTECTION

CAPTCHA

CAPTCHA-style checks can add a verification step before members gain access.

Setup notes

If CAPTCHA is supported by your Ghosty version, configure it alongside verification.

  • Explain how to complete the challenge.
  • Provide a way to retry if something fails.
  • Make sure staff can help members who encounter problems.
PROTECTION

Honeypot

A restricted channel can help detect activity in areas members should not access.

Use carefully

Make sure the channel permissions match your intended detection design.

  • Review who can see the channel.
  • Keep staff informed about the detection process.
  • Check how events are recorded and reviewed.
Confirm the exact behaviour in your installed version before relying on automatic moderation.
PROTECTION

DM Protection

Guidance for reducing unwanted direct-message activity associated with your community.

Protect member trust

Explain what your protection features can and cannot prevent, and tell members where they can report suspicious messages.

  • Never ask members to share passwords or authentication codes.
  • Give members a clear way to report suspicious activity.
  • Be clear about any automated moderation actions.

REFERENCE

Permissions

Give Ghosty only the permissions it needs, and keep sensitive commands limited to trusted staff.

Permissions checklist

  • Review the permissions requested during installation.
  • Position the bot role appropriately for its moderation actions.
  • Restrict setup and lockdown commands to trusted staff.
  • Check channel-specific permissions if the bot cannot respond.
  • Review staff access regularly.
Discord role hierarchy can prevent a bot from moderating members whose highest role is equal to or above the bot's role.
REFERENCE

Troubleshooting

Solutions to common problems when setting up a Discord bot.

Ghosty is offline

Check your hosting service and review the application logs for startup errors.

Commands do not appear

Check that command registration completed successfully and that you invited the correct application.

A command does not respond

Check bot permissions, application logs and whether the command exists in the deployed version.

Moderation actions fail

Review Discord permissions and the bot's role position.

Still stuck?

Join the support server and provide the error message, what you were doing, and any relevant logs. Never share your bot token.
